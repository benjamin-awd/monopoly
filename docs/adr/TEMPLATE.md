# NNNN. Short decision title

## Context

The forces at play: what problem, what constraints, what makes this
non-obvious. State the situation, not the answer.

## Decision

What we are doing, stated plainly and in the present tense.

## Alternatives considered

is the part a diff cannot preserve.

## Consequences
